# Privacy Policy – Draxoin AI

Last updated: August 29, 2026

## 1. About Draxoin AI

Draxoin AI is a Discord bot that provides AI-powered responses to users through the `!ai` command.

Example:

`!ai What is the capital of France?`

## 2. Information We Process

When you use Draxoin AI, the bot may process:

- Your Discord username or user ID
- The Discord server ID
- The message you send using the `!ai` command
- Information required to provide the AI response

Draxoin AI does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information is used only to:

- Process your AI request
- Generate and send a response
- Operate and maintain the Discord bot
- Prevent abuse or misuse of the bot

## 4. Data Storage

Draxoin AI does not intentionally permanently store the content of your AI questions unless this is necessary for the operation of the bot.

## 5. Third-Party Services

AI requests may be processed by third-party AI services used by Draxoin AI to generate responses.

## 6. Data Deletion

If you believe that personal information associated with your use of Draxoin AI has been stored, you may contact the bot owner and request its deletion.
